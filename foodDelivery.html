Food Delivery App – Complete Mobile Solution

Food Delivery is a complete mobile solution built with React Native CLI & Expo, featuring two applications:

User App – for browsing restaurants, adding meals to cart, placing orders.

Delivery App – for drivers/delivery agents to manage assigned orders, update delivery status.

Multi-language support: Arabic & English with instant switch.

The project includes 30+ ready-to-use screens with modern UI and seamless navigation.

Perfect for launching your own food delivery business or learning React Native development.

Food Delivery App – Modern & Flexible

It comes with dual language support (Arabic & English) for a global audience and Dark/Light mode to match user preferences. Designed with 30+ ready-to-use screens featuring clean UI and smooth navigation.

User App allows customers to explore restaurants, browse menus, add meals to their cart, place orders, and track deliveries in real-time with an intuitive interface.

Delivery App empowers drivers with smart tools to manage assigned orders, view pickup & drop-off locations, and update delivery status seamlessly.

Perfect solution for launching your own food delivery business, or as a learning project to master React Native development with modern best practices.
